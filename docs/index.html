Method and limits

  • Data: 101,766 inpatient encounters from 130 US hospitals, 1999 to 2008 (Strack et al., 2014). Deaths and hospice discharges are removed and one encounter is kept per patient.
  • Model: gradient boosted trees (XGBoost) on 42 features available at discharge, compared with a logistic regression baseline. The trees are exported to JSON and scored in this page with plain JavaScript.
  • An AUROC in the mid 0.60s is typical for readmission models built on administrative data. Many causes of readmission, such as social support and housing, are not recorded.
  • The data is old and from the US. A model like this must be retrained and validated on local, recent data before any clinical use.
